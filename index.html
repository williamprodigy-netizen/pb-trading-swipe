What matters

They name the area code that will call you &mdash; steal this

The thank-you H1 is: “Wait, You're Not Done Yet! Our Team Will Be Calling You From A +1 (305) Phone Number ASAP To Walk You Through How To Get Onboarded.” Suprahuman warns that an unknown number will text. PB Trading goes one better and gives the actual area code, which turns an unknown caller into a recognised one. This is the single cheapest speed-to-lead fix in the whole swipe file.

The proof is a streak, not a total

“862 days in a row” and “7 new traders every single week”. A total (“5,000 traders funded”) is a claim about the past. A streak is a claim about consistency, and it is falsifiable — which is exactly why it reads as more honest. It also implies the result is systematic rather than lucky.

The enemy is a behaviour they already know they have

“Strategy hopping & emotional trading” is not a problem the copy has to teach — every failing trader recognises it instantly and is ashamed of it. The hook names the self-diagnosis rather than the outcome. Our equivalent is naming the loop, not the income.

Fifteen testimonial videos, all on the thank-you page

None on the VSL page. The proof is spent after the application, on someone who has already committed — which is where doubt actually lives. Same instinct as Dlucs and Ecom Accelerator.
